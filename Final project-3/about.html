About the Artist

Hi, I'm Jennifer Golya!

Art can be expressed in many ways whether it be through visual, musical, culinary, theatrical, literary, or architectural forms. Throughout my life, I have always gravitated to creative activities where I can express myself through drawing & painting. I have either concentrated my career around the arts or participated in side projects as an outlet to create. Drawing & painting is very therapeutic for me and it’s where I am happiest. Hence the inception of The Art Cart - a local Cleveland business that focuses on enhancing brick & mortar entrances with your specific products & services in mind.

My high school and college education concentrated on the visual arts including personalizing gifts with hand painted designs as well as creating promotional graphics for local businesses. This complimented my degree in Advertising and Marketing. During my most recent creative position as an art studio manager and instructor, I became interested in the craft of window painting. With my marketing & advertising background, I began customizing bold & bright images for local businesses that I designed to promote the specifics of each business. In the age of big box stores & internet consumerism, window art catches the eye of customers who may not have otherwise noticed a storefront on a busy street. Window art compliments a business’ individuality and gives a commercial building its unique look to set it apart from the others. While painting each storefront, I find myself truly emersed in creating custom art that compliments each business. It would be my pleasure to design vibrant storefront art for your business that will attract more traffic to your door.
